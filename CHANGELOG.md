# Changelog

All notable changes to Deep Researcher are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

### Added
- Adaptive research planning based on question complexity.
- Focused, Comprehensive, and Exhaustive research modes.
- Multi-pass research and coverage tracking.
- Source discovery and source-quality evaluation.
- Source diversity and independent evidence triangulation.
- Primary-source verification for important claims.
- Quantitative claim verification, including definitions, denominators, populations, and time periods.
- Counterevidence and competing-explanation analysis.
- Recency awareness and publication-status verification.
- Explicit uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations.
- Structured DOCX report generation for substantial research tasks.
- Report quality checks covering citations, claims, structure, and document formatting.
- Progress updates during extended research workflows.

### Quality Principles
- No fabricated sources, citations, quotations, or research activity.
- Prefer primary evidence over derivative reporting.
- Distinguish established facts from interpretations and hypotheses.
- Ensure conclusions are proportionate to the available evidence.

### Status
Experimental. Execution testing across multiple research domains is still required.
