# Contributing to Deep Researcher

Contributions are welcome. The goal is to make Deep Researcher more rigorous, accurate, reliable, and useful across research domains.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork this repository.
2. Create a branch for your changes.
3. Make focused changes to `SKILL.md` or supporting documentation.
4. Test your changes using realistic research questions.
5. Submit a pull request explaining the changes and test results.

## Contribution Guidelines

### Research methodology
- Prefer primary sources and authoritative evidence.
- Verify important claims against original sources.
- Distinguish facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and unknowns.
- Include relevant counterevidence and competing explanations.
- Make uncertainty and research limitations explicit.
- Never fabricate sources, citations, quotations, or research activity.

### Source quality
- Assess source authority, methodology, independence, recency, and potential bias.
- Avoid treating multiple reports repeating the same source as independent corroboration.
- Verify publication status and dates where relevant.
- Preserve quantitative definitions, populations, denominators, and time periods.

### Skill improvements
- Keep instructions clear, actionable, and internally consistent.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity and repetitive instructions.
- Preserve adaptive research depth.
- Test changes across different topics and research domains.

### Report quality
- Keep reports structured and decision-oriented.
- Use tables and charts when they improve understanding.
- Ensure citations support the claims they accompany.
- Check document formatting, pagination, and references before submitting changes.

## Reporting Issues

Include:
- The research prompt used.
- Expected behaviour.
- Actual behaviour.
- Relevant output or error messages.
- Sources or claims involved, where applicable.

## Pull Requests

Keep pull requests focused. Explain:
- What changed.
- Why it changed.
- How you tested it.
- Known limitations or unresolved issues.

By contributing, you agree that your contributions will be released under the repository's MIT License.
